MODULE: roles  (modules/roles/)
===============================
  /addvol <user>    Volunteer  - 30 quota, direct indexing without approval.
  /addprom <user>   Promoter   - 20 quota.
  /addmod <user>    Moderator  - 40 quota, manages request statuses.
  /addadmin <user>  Admin      - unlimited quota and full admin rights (Owner only).
<user> = numeric ID, @username (must have used the bot) or a replied-to message.
Only Admin/Owner may assign roles. The target gets a DM, the action is logged to the user log
topic and the "role_updated" template is shown. Base quota changes at once; referral/gift bonus
quota is preserved. Quota defaults come from config (promoter/volunteer/moderator_quota_default).
Owners are defined by ADMIN_IDS / OWNER_IDS and cannot be demoted by commands.
